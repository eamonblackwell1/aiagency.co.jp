### What Is an AI Phone Agent?

An AI phone agent is a voice-based artificial intelligence system that answers incoming phone calls on behalf of your business. Think of it as a virtual receptionist that never takes a break, never calls in sick, and never puts a caller on hold.

When a customer calls your salon or clinic, the AI phone agent picks up, greets the caller, and handles the conversation. It can answer frequently asked questions — business hours, pricing, location, available services — and guide the caller through the booking process. If the call requires human attention, it seamlessly transfers to your staff.

AI Agency Japan's phone agent is built specifically for the Japanese market. The voice quality is native-level Japanese, not the robotic or awkward tone that plagues many AI voice solutions. This matters enormously in Japan, where customer service expectations are exceptionally high and callers will judge your business within seconds.
