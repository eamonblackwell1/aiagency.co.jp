## The Hidden Cost of Slow DM Replies

Think about how your salon currently handles Instagram DMs. Most likely, a stylist or manager checks messages between appointments, replying in batches a few times a day. Sometimes messages get lost in the scroll. Sometimes a reply comes hours — or even a day — later.

Research consistently shows that the faster a business responds to an inquiry, the more likely that inquiry converts to a booking. A reply within minutes dramatically outperforms a reply within hours. For salons in competitive areas like Harajuku, Omotesando, and Shibuya, speed is a real differentiator.

The problem isn't that salon teams don't care about DMs. It's that they're physically doing other things — cutting hair, mixing color, managing the front desk. DMs fall to the bottom of the priority list, and bookings fall through the cracks.
